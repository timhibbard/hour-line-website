Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Hour Line: Zone Compass ("Hour Line") is an app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch made by Tim Hibbard. The short version: Hour Line doesn't collect any information about you.

Location

Hour Line uses your device's location and compass to find nearby time zones and point to them. This happens entirely on your device, using time zone boundaries that ship inside the app. Your location is never sent to me or to anyone else.

If you turn on crossing or approaching alerts, Hour Line uses iOS's low-power location features in the background to notice when you near or cross a time zone border. That also stays on your device. You can turn alerts off at any time in the app's settings, and change location access in iOS Settings.

Driving directions

When you open a zone's driving directions, Hour Line asks Apple Maps for a route from your current location to that border, the same as any Maps search. That request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Hour Line Pro is a one-time purchase made through Apple. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; I never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with the App Store whether Pro is unlocked.

What Hour Line doesn't do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party SDKs. Settings and the last location result are stored on your device so the app and its widgets open quickly, and are deleted if you delete the app.

Children

Hour Line doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
