Support

Found a bug, a border in the wrong place, or have an idea? Email timhibbard@gmail.com. It helps to include your device, iOS version and roughly where you were.

Why is there a zone just off the coast?

The oceans have time zones too, one per hour of longitude, and the open sea doesn't observe daylight saving time. In summer, the sea next to a coast is often an hour off from the land.

Why doesn't the arrow point at the nearest town in that zone?

Each arrow points to the closest point on the border, in a straight line. The driving directions take you to just past that point, which isn't always the quickest place to cross by car.

Why are some zones far away?

An arrow means that's the first time zone you'd reach heading straight in that direction, even across water. Zones more than 1,000 miles away are hidden unless you turn on "Show zones over 1,000 mi away" in Settings.

The compass seems off

Move away from metal and electronics, and wave your phone in a figure eight to recalibrate it. Without a compass (some iPads), the dial is shown with north at the top.

I'm not getting crossing alerts

Alerts are part of Hour Line Pro. They need notifications turned on and location access set to "Always" for Hour Line in iOS Settings. iOS checks your location with low-power signals, so an alert can arrive a minute or two after you cross.

Restoring Pro

Open Settings in Hour Line and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.
